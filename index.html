You Are Mine.

I came to college for you. The exam was optional, but I came anyway.

Then I got hurt. I didn't even realise how badly I was bleeding until I saw your face.

You froze for a few seconds. Then you came running.

"And then came that hug."

Don't Go Yet → You're Back 🧳

You came to my house to help me pack.

You were sad because you couldn't see me for a week. You cried and asked me to stay.

I waited near my house. You saw me.

"And that hug…"

You hugged me so hard like you were making sure I wasn't going anywhere again.
